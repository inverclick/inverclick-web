# Imágenes de Nosotros

Generadas con la herramienta integrada ImageGen a partir de la referencia visual y optimizadas con Sharp a WebP (calidad 85). Son imágenes ilustrativas, no fotografías del equipo ni de clientes.

## `public/about-us/familia.webp`

Prompt final:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: website hero background, landscape 2.5:1 aspect ratio. Create a polished realistic lifestyle photograph for a Colombian property website. A happy Latino mother, father and 8-year-old daughter sit closely together on a cream sofa in a bright airy modern home, smiling naturally and looking slightly upward. Father in cream sweater and blue jeans, mother long brown hair in white blouse, daughter in ivory knit sweater. Composition: family occupies the RIGHT HALF of frame, heads fully visible with space above, legs cropped at bottom. LEFT HALF is a soft almost white empty interior for text overlay, continuous seamless background. Soft window daylight, warm whites, beige, small green plant far right, natural skin texture. No text, logos, watermarks or graphics.
